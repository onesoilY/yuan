About

Hi, I’m Joel, my legal name is Yuan Xiao, but feel free to call me either.

I’m an Interactive Arts & Technology student at Simon Fraser University. I specialize in designing interactive experiences, creating immersive narrative-driven games, and building innovative websites and apps. Explore my work on the portfolio page.

As a Game Interaction Designer, I’m passionate about building interactive environments and intuitive user experiences that balance clarity, aesthetics, and usability. By blending creativity with technology and applying design thinking, rapid prototyping, and iterative testing, I deliver high-quality digital solutions that captivate and engage users. I bring a keen eye for detail and a collaborative mindset to every project, and I look forward to refining these skills alongside a dedicated team.
