Kinetic Typography

Project Overview

Project Goal:

  • To create an engaging kinetic typography animation for “I’m with You” by Avril Lavigne.
  • Synchronizing expressive text movements with the song’s rhythm and emotion while applying core animation principles to enhance visual storytelling.

My Role: Designer & Animator

Built With:

  • After Effect (motion graphics)
  • Premiere Pro (video editing)

Work Time: 1 Month (July 2023 - August 2023)

Showcase:

Project Description

Kinetic Typography Animator – Music Video for Avril Lavigne’s "I’m with You"

In this project, I designed and animated a Kinetic Typography sequence for Avril Lavigne’s "I’m with You" using Adobe After Effects, focusing on expressive text movements synchronized with the music’s rhythm and emotion.

  • Design and Animation Approach:
    • Applied core animation principles, including Squash and Stretch, Anticipation, Follow-through, Slow in/Slow out (easing), and Secondary Action, ensuring fluid, natural motion and visually compelling transitions.
    • Carefully controlled position, size, velocity, and relative scale of text elements to create rhythmic patterns and emphasize lyrical beats.
    • Integrated linear and non-linear directional movements to highlight key phrases dynamically.
  • Visual Composition and Motion Choreography:
    • Maintained a clear center of interest, using contrast between still and moving elements to guide the viewer’s focus.
    • Aligned the relationship of movement to frame, ensuring text flowed seamlessly within the video’s visual space without overpowering the background elements.
    • Balanced text animations with the song’s mood, adjusting timing and amplitude of movements to match emotional highs and lows.
  • Creative Integration and Finishing:
    • Incorporated digital photography and vector image elements, enriching the composition with layered textures and visual depth.
    • Synchronized transitions with audio cues, blending text and imagery into a cohesive visual narrative.

Takeaways

This project enhanced my ability to synchronize kinetic typography with music, ensuring expressive and fluid motion through key animation principles. I refined my skills in visual composition and motion choreography, maintaining a clear focal point while balancing movement and stillness. Additionally, I deepened my expertise in After Effects by seamlessly integrating text, imagery, and audio into a cohesive visual narrative.
