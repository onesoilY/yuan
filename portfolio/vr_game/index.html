Project Description

A Deeply Immersive VR Journey

Beyond the Horizon is an interactive VR experience that immerses players in the emotional journey of a man grappling with the loss of a close friend. As the protagonist navigates his grief, his interactions with objects and environments gradually reveal memories, while his bond with a dog offers a path toward healing.

My role in this project was centered on 3D environmental design and interactive object implementation, ensuring that every visual and interactive element contributed to the overarching narrative. I designed and modeled the protagonist’s bedroom, key interactive objects, and environmental transitions, aiming to reflect his shifting emotional state through spatial composition, lighting, and object placement.

To bring this vision to life, I was responsible for creating optimized 3D assets in Maya, focusing on clean topology and efficient UV mapping to ensure smooth rendering in VR. I also worked on object interaction mechanics in Unity, implementing C# scripts to allow players to engage with key elements like photographs, mirrors, and a deflated swim tube—each designed to subtly convey the protagonist’s internal struggles. In addition, I collaborated with my team to integrate lighting and sound elements that enhanced immersion, ensuring that the visual storytelling aligned with the game’s emotional depth.

Takeaways

This project reinforced the significance of environmental storytelling in VR—how spaces and objects can communicate emotions just as effectively as dialogue or cinematics. I learned how topology, UV mapping, and texture optimization are critical for maintaining performance in immersive experiences, and how interactive object mechanics can subtly guide the player’s perception and engagement with the narrative. Additionally, the use of lighting and sound design to shape emotional tone became a key takeaway, influencing my approach to future projects.

Moving forward, I aim to further refine character interaciton and animations to enhance realism and expand player-driven choices, ensuring that interactive narratives are not only visually compelling but also deeply immersive on an emotional level.
