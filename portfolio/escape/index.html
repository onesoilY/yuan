Escape

Script-adapted Board Game

Published on Mar 14, 2025

Estimated reading time: 5 minutes

Project Overview

Project Goal:

  • Transform a self-written post-apocalyptic script titled “Redemption” into a highly interactive multiplayer board game that preserves the script’s tense atmosphere and internal conflicts.
  • By integrating card mechanics, map design, hidden roles, and cooperative gameplay, the project enhances players’ immersion and emotional engagement.

My Role: Game Designer & Video Editor

Collaborators:

  • Jianting Liang – Card Designer & Video Editor

Work Time: 2 Months (October 2024 - December 2024)

Project Description

Reimagining Redemption as a Board Game Escape

Escape was originally a post-apocalyptic script that follows Oliver’s struggle after a viral outbreak, depicting scarce resources, team trust issues, and harsh survival conditions. When adapting it into a board game, we focused on the following core elements:

  • Betrayal vs. Teamwork

    A hidden traitor is a major tension point in the script, so our game features a hidden-role system that forces players to question each other’s motives, creating a tense, uncertain atmosphere.

  • Resources & Risks

    The original story highlights resource scarcity and high-stakes environments. We translated this into a “Single Phase” and “Collective Phase,” with the map divided into the City, Creek, and Coast—each with unique risks and rewards.

  • Nightfall & Dawnlight

    Taking actions at Night increases the danger posed by the monster “FEAR” and requires players to reveal their cards. The Daytime option is safer, but yields fewer resources. This mechanic mirrors the script’s tension surrounding day and night in the post-apocalyptic setting.

Design Process

1

Event Cards

Venturing into a Post-Apocalyptic Realm: Event Cards-Single Route Cards above and Collective Route Cards below

These event cards form the backbone of the game's tension and unpredictability. Each card introduces a unique scenario—ranging from resource finds to ambushes, mutated creatures, or sabotage attempts—that compels players to adapt their strategies on the fly. Designed with distinct color themes to indicate varying degrees of danger and different times of day.

I was responsible for crafting and fine-tuning the narrative structure behind each event, ensuring it not only fits the overall storyline but also adds depth to the game’s mechanics. From ideating card concepts to writing event descriptions, I worked to embed strategic challenges that reinforce the game’s post-apocalyptic atmosphere. Additionally, I oversaw the design and testing process, balancing the cards’ effects, ultimately heightening player engagement and emotional investment.

2

Character Cards

Character Cards: Defining Roles, Trust, and Betrayal

Each character card represents a key role in the game, shaping player interactions and survival strategies. From the deceptive Traitor to the resilient Survivors, and the central figure Oliver, these cards influence combat abilities, alliances, and betrayals. The character illustrations were created by Katrina, while the card frames were designed by Jianting.

I developed the their traits and abilities aligned with the game’s mechanics and narrative. I also refined role balance through playtesting, making key character impactful in driving strategy and storytelling.

3

Instruction Book

Comprehensive Game Guide: Rules, Strategy, and Survival

This instruction book provides players with an in-depth understanding of game mechanics, from character roles and action cards to survival strategies and victory conditions. The layout is designed by Jianting, it ensures clarity in navigating the Single Phase and Collective Phase, emphasizing decision-making under pressure, hidden identities, and resource management. The guide also introduces the FEAR Mechanic, reinforcing tension through movement rules that heighten player urgency.

I assisted in structuring and refining the game instruction book, ensuring they aligned with the narrative. Additionally, I helped streamline explanations for accessibility, making complex interactions more intuitive for players.

4

Map

Post-Apocalyptic Game Map: A Journey Through Risk and Survival

Illustrated by Katrina, brings to life a world of scarcity and danger, where players must navigate three key zones—Abandoned City, Silent Creek, and Barren Coast—each with distinct risks and resources. Throughout the map, 12 campsites serve as critical decision points, with bonfires appearing every two camps to allow players to attack suspected traitors. Adding to the intensity, FEAR relentlessly pursues players, forcing them to strategize their movements while balancing survival and trust.

I designed the level mechanics, initial sketches, and concept, structuring how players move, interact with campsites, trigger bonfire attacks, and evade FEAR’s pursuit. My focus was on balancing risk and reward, ensuring every movement carried weight, while Katrina brought the world to life through her artwork.

Takeaways

Through this project, I honed my ability to write compelling scripts and seamlessly adapt narrative elements into engaging gameplay experiences. My skillset expanded to include level design and the construction of immersive worlds, ensuring that the storyline and mechanics worked in harmony to captivate and challenge players.

Additionally, I developed a thorough understanding of video production, leveraging various filming techniques and camera angles to bring key moments to life—both in gameplay demonstrations and promotional material. By merging narrative design, game design, and video editing expertise, I am able to create cohesive, dynamic projects that resonate with audiences and elevate the overall experience.
