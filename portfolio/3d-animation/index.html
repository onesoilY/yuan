Project Description

Bringing Set Sail Again to Life

I played a key role in designing and optimizing the 3D assets and animation for Set Sail Again, ensuring a visually compelling and emotionally resonant experience. By carefully modeling, texturing, and lighting key elements such as the lighthouse, Milo the cat, and the captain, I brought the narrative to life with attention to detail and artistic cohesion. Utilizing Maya, I maintained clean topology and precise UV mapping to achieve realistic textures and smooth animation. The project's stylized yet expressive characters and immersive coastal environment enhance storytelling depth, reinforcing the story’s themes of redemption and personal growth.

Design & Develop

1

Lighthouse

  • Modeling:
    • Combined online references and personal sketches for realistic proportions and details.
    • Utilized symmetry tools to streamline the design.
    • Maintained clean topology and avoided N-gons for smooth rendering.
  • Texturing:
    • Applied precise UV mapping for seamless material application.
    • Used materials such as brick and concrete for a realistic look.
  • Lighting and Effects:
    • Implemented an HDRI lighting setup to enhance realism.
    • Carefully crafted the lighthouse beam for a striking nighttime effect.
2

Captain

  • Modeling:
    • Modeled in Maya with a stylized yet expressive design, conveying wisdom and resilience.
    • Focused on detailed facial features, including the beard and hat, to enhance personality and storytelling depth.
  • UV Mapping:
    • Conducted precise UV unwrapping to ensure seamless texture application.
    • Minimized distortion to maintain high texture quality for clothing and skin.
  • Texturing:
    • Emphasized texture details, highlighting realistic clothing materials and skin features.
    • Enhanced visual storytelling with thoughtful material choices.
  • Rigging:
    • Created a complete skeleton for posing and animation.
    • Applied skin weighting with paint skin weights for smooth deformation, ensuring natural movements around joints and facial expressions.
3

Kitty

  • Modeling:
    • Modeled in Maya, focusing on smooth topology for clean deformation.
    • Used symmetry tools to ensure balanced proportions and maintain a consistent style.
    • Emphasized expressive facial details to enhance the character’s personality.
  • UV Mapping:
    • Unwrapped UVs carefully to ensure efficient and seamless texture application.
    • Minimized seams and distortion for a clean texture projection.
  • Texturing:
    • Highlighted detailed fur patterns and fabric elements to enrich visual appeal.
    • Applied appropriate materials to ensure texture consistency.
  • Rigging:
    • Created a basic skeleton for posing and animation.
    • Applied paint skin weights for smooth deformation, focusing on natural facial.
